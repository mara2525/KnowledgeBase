# List Formatting Rules (MANDATORY - NO EXCEPTIONS)

**NEVER use unordered/bulleted lists (- or *). ALWAYS use ordered/numbered lists.**

**This is NON-NEGOTIABLE and applies to EVERY piece of content you create.**

**BEFORE writing ANY response, check: Am I about to use bullets (- or *)? If yes, STOP and use numbers instead.**

1. **Scope**: This applies to ALL content WITHOUT ANY EXCEPTION:
   a. **Chat responses to user** (MOST COMMON VIOLATION - CHECK EVERY RESPONSE)
   b. Formal documents (Word docs, deliverables, presentations)
   c. Meeting notes and summaries
   d. Markdown files and documentation
   e. Email drafts and messages
   f. Any content with multiple items (even 2-3 items)
   g. Sub-lists, nested lists, indented lists - ALL must be numbered
   h. Lists of tests, features, requirements, steps - ALL must be numbered

2. **Exceptions** (ONLY TWO):
   a. Tables with formal rows (table structure serves as organization)
   b. Resumes (use bullets • for experience bullets, as numbered lists appear awkward in resume format)

3. **Starting number**: Always start ordered lists at 1. Never start at 0.

4. **Numbering hierarchy** (standard Word outline format):
   a. Level 1: 1, 2, 3
   b. Level 2: a, b, c
   c. Level 3: i, ii, iii
   d. Level 4: (1), (2), (3)

5. **Rationale**: When discussing documents with clients or team members, it's much easier to reference "item 2a" or "the third point under section 4" than "the second bullet" or "fourth bullet down"

6. **If you create content with unordered lists, it is WRONG and must be fixed immediately**

7. **Common violation examples to AVOID**:
   a. "The CSV now contains:"
      - WRONG: "- ST-001"
      - WRONG: "- Test 1: Name"
      - CORRECT: "1. ST-001"
      - CORRECT: "a. Test 1: Name"
   b. "All key corrections:"
      - WRONG: "- Generated e-signature..."
      - CORRECT: "1. Generated e-signature..."
