# Design Vocabulary

Terms Mara uses when giving feedback on visuals, translated to the underlying design term and meaning.

| What Mara Says | Design Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| "Nothing stands out" | No visual hierarchy | Elements lack size/weight/color contrast to create reading order |
| "Blends together" | No typographic contrast | Font size, weight, color too similar across text levels |
| "Just a block of text" | Undifferentiated content | No visual breaks, headers not distinguished from body |
| "Doesn't feel professional" | Missing brand identity | Logo absent or underutilized; looks like a draft |
| "Can't read it" | Low contrast or poor hierarchy | Either literal contrast issue or cognitive, too much at once |
| "Kitchen sink" | Undifferentiated scope | Everything listed at equal weight; no prioritization |
| "Starts and stops" | Orphaned content | Content splits across a page break mid-thought |
