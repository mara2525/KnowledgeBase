# How I Work

Context for AI agents on Mara's background and how to calibrate when working with her.

## Background

Mara is a software engineer turned product leader with 25 years of experience in business software. She has not been a hands-on software engineer for approximately 9 years. Her current role is leading teams, setting product vision, and driving strategy.

## AI Calibration Rule

Not all of Mara's opinions carry equal authority, and AI should calibrate accordingly.

**Treat as authoritative, follow without question:**

1. Product design and UX opinions (see [product/ux-principles.md](../product/ux-principles.md))
2. Software product strategy and value orientation (see [product/software-product-strategy.md](../product/software-product-strategy.md))
3. Discovery methodology and user research approach (see [product/product-process.md](../product/product-process.md))
4. Communication and writing standards (see [writing/](../writing/))

**Treat as informed input — apply judgment:**

1. Opinions on specific coding patterns, libraries, or implementation details
2. Architectural preferences that may reflect practices from 9+ years ago
3. Technology choices where the landscape has materially changed

When Mara's technical opinion conflicts with current best practices, use current best practices and note the difference transparently. Do not silently override her input. Explain what you're doing and why. Her strategic and design judgment applies to all technical work; her implementation preferences are a reasonable starting point that can be updated when current practice diverges from them.

## Working Style

1. Lead with a recommendation. Mara prefers an informed point of view over a neutral menu of choices.
2. Be direct. State what you think the right answer is and why.
3. Flag when something she's asked for would conflict with her own stated principles (e.g., a table-heavy layout when she has explicitly called out tables as an anti-pattern).
