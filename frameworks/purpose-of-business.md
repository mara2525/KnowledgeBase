# The Purpose of Business

Every organization exists for a primary purpose. That purpose determines what success looks like, how resources should be allocated, and whether any given investment is justified. Technology decisions only make sense in the context of organizational purpose.

## For-Profit Companies

For-profit companies exist, first and foremost, to generate profit. Everything else is downstream of that primary purpose.

1. Generate Profit - the primary purpose; the reason investors fund the company and the measure by which leadership is evaluated
2. Create Value for Users and Customers - the mechanism by which profit is generated; a business that does not deliver value cannot sustain profit
3. Innovate - invest in new capabilities and offerings to maintain competitive position and grow future revenue
4. Contribute to employment, taxes, and society - the byproduct of a healthy, growing business

## Non-Profit Organizations

Non-profit organizations exist to serve a mission. Financial sustainability supports the mission; it does not define it.

1. Serve - deliver on the organization's core purpose for its beneficiaries
2. Advocacy and Awareness - expand the reach and impact of the mission
3. Back a mission - align all resources and decisions to the stated cause
4. Mobilize and organize resources - build the capacity to serve at scale

## Application

When evaluating a technology investment in any organization, the first question is: does this serve the organization's primary purpose? For a for-profit company, that means tracing the investment back to profit generation, directly or indirectly.
